## Mandatory reporting safeguards
- Separate lab evidence from HanTech production claims.
- Distinguish confirmed exposure/configuration from CVE candidate and untested impact.
- Include the exact target, command/method, timestamp, evidence path and SHA-256 for material claims.
- No exploit-confirmed claim where no exploit was performed.
- Identify unresolved dependencies and evidence gaps.
- Individual report contributions must remain attributable to each student.
