CAPSTONE PROJECT 138 — RESEARCH SOURCE AND APPLICABILITY LOG
Date: 8 November 2026 (Africa/Lagos)
Purpose: Record authoritative public sources used to prepare the vulnerability, risk, controls, GRC and cloud workstream drafts.
Nature of evidence: Public-source desk research and project-lab evidence synthesis. This file is not a live control test, legal opinion, cloud tenant review or production assessment.

- Samba upstream CVE-2007-2447 advisory: https://www.samba.org/samba/security/CVE-2007-2447.html
  Use: upstream affected version range and username-map-script prerequisite. Target-specific Ubuntu package patch provenance remains unresolved; the target is not called exploit-confirmed.
- Ubuntu Samba USN-460-1: https://ubuntu.com/security/notices/USN-460-1
  Use: vendor patch/advisory context for Samba; do not rely on upstream version alone.
- Ubuntu CVE-2007-2447 tracker: https://ubuntu.com/security/CVE-2007-2447
  Use: release-specific affected/fixed context; current maintained-release status does not settle the historical unmaintained Hardy binary.
