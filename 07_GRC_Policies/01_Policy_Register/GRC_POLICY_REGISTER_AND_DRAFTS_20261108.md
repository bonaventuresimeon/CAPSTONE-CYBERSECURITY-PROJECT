# GRC Policy Pack — Register and Draft Framework
Status: Starter pack; Task 6 owner/team confirmation required. Drafts below are policy outlines, not approved organisational policies.
## Policy register (minimum four)
| ID | Policy | Objective and minimum requirements | Owner / approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| POL-01 | Information Security Policy | Governance, roles, risk ownership, acceptable use, compliance, exceptions, annual review | CISO / Executive |
| POL-02 | Access Control and Identity Management Policy | Joiner/mover/leaver, unique IDs, MFA, privileged access, reviews, service accounts | IT/IAM lead |
| POL-03 | Vulnerability and Patch Management Policy | Asset coverage, severity-based SLAs, scanning, validation, remediation, exceptions, retesting | Security operations |
| POL-04 | Incident Response and Reporting Policy | Detection, triage, containment, evidence preservation, escalation, notification, lessons learned | Incident response lead |
| POL-05 | Data Classification and Handling Policy | Classification, storage, transmission, retention, disposal, encryption, secrets | Data owner |
| POL-06 | Backup, Recovery and Business Continuity Policy | Backup scope, encryption, retention, restore tests, RTO/RPO, resilience | IT operations |
## Common policy clauses
Purpose; scope; definitions; policy statements; roles and responsibilities; control requirements; exceptions and risk acceptance; monitoring and enforcement; incident/escalation; records/evidence; review cadence; approval and effective date.
## Draft policy requirements
POL-01: All systems and data must have accountable owners; risk decisions must be documented; security exceptions require named approval and expiry; policy compliance is monitored and reviewed at least annually.
POL-02: Access must be uniquely attributable, least-privilege and role-based; privileged accounts must be separately controlled; MFA is required for remote and privileged access where technically supported; access is reviewed periodically and promptly revoked on termination.
POL-03: Assets must be inventoried and assigned risk-based patch SLAs; critical exploitable exposures are prioritised; emergency changes are logged; exceptions require compensating controls, owner and expiry; remediation is verified by retest.
POL-04: Events must be reported through defined channels; responders preserve logs and chain of custody; containment must be authorised; legal/regulatory notification decisions are made by designated management/legal roles; post-incident reviews generate tracked actions.
POL-05: Data owners classify information; sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest where appropriate; secrets must not be stored in public web roots or source repositories; retention and secure disposal are defined.
POL-06: Backups are protected from unauthorised access and ransomware; restore tests validate recoverability; RTO/RPO are approved by business owners; recovery procedures are documented and exercised.
